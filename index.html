Niladri Shekhar Dutt

I'm a second-year ELLIS PhD candidate (start: Oct'24) at University College London (UCL), sponsored by Adobe Research. I'm advised by Niloy Mitra (UCL) and Duygu Ceylan (Adobe). I'm currently interning again as a Research Scientist at Adobe Research with Jakub Fiser on video generation.

My research studies visual representation and multimodal learning — how the visual world should be represented in tokens, if generative vision models represent structured plans, and whether explicit structured intermediate representations improve compositional generalization in foundation models.

Previously Research Scientist Intern at Adobe (with Xuelin Chen) · Research Intern at NVIDIA · Visiting Student at UC Berkeley · Research Engineer at Ready Player Me (acquired by Netflix) · Founding Engineer at NFTPort (raised $26M, now Galadriel)
