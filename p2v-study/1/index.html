Which video follows the description?

Thank you for taking part. We are testing methods that generate a video from a written description of a scene. In each comparison you see two videos, A and B, made from the same description by two different methods. Your task is to tell us which one does what the description asks.

What you will see

  • The two videos, side by side. They are about 5 seconds long, play together and replay automatically. Use Pause, Restart, ½ speed and the slider to look closely.
  • A short description of the scene, the list of things in it and what each one does, the camera, and the full detailed description both videos were made from.
  • The critical instruction, highlighted in yellow: the one thing the shot is really about, for example a direction of motion, a turn, an effect, an object hidden and revealed, a collision, or a camera move. When the camera moves, the camera instruction is highlighted in blue.

The three questions

  1. Which video follows the description better? The right things, in the right places, doing what the description says, in the right order.
  2. Which video looks more realistic and natural? Believable motion, shapes that stay solid, physics that make sense, a clean image.
  3. Which video follows the critical instruction better? Judge only the highlighted instruction.

Please keep in mind

  • Things the description does not mention (extra objects, the background, colours of minor details) do not count for or against a video.
  • Both videos may be imperfect. Choose the one that is closer; there is no "same" option.
  • Answers unlock once both videos have played to the end once. Watch them several times if you need to.
  • Please use a laptop or desktop screen, in a quiet moment. There are 30 comparisons, about 20 minutes. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can take a break and come back.
