Which edit follows the instruction?

Thank you for taking part. We are testing methods that edit a real video from a written instruction, for example "the dog runs in from the right" or "the camera slowly zooms in". In each comparison you see the original video and two edited versions, A and B, made from the same instruction by two different methods. Your task is to tell us which edit does what the instruction asks.

What you will see

  • The original video (grey frame) and the two edits, A and B. They are about 5 seconds long, play together and replay automatically. Use Pause, Restart, ½ speed and the slider to compare them moment by moment.
  • The edit instruction, highlighted in yellow: the one change that was asked for.

The three questions

  1. Which edit carries out the instruction better? The requested change happens, to the right thing, in the right direction, place, amount and timing. An edit that leaves the video as it was, changes the wrong thing or does a different change has not followed the instruction, however clean it looks.
  2. Which edit keeps everything else closer to the original? The people, animals and objects keep their look, the setting stays the same, and what was not asked to change moves as in the original. Changes that follow from the edit (a place goes dark when the light leaves it; everything gets larger in a zoom) are expected.
  3. Which edit looks more realistic and natural? Believable motion, shapes that stay solid, no flicker, smearing or things appearing and disappearing.

Please keep in mind

  • Both edits may be imperfect. Choose the one that is closer; there is no "same" option.
  • Answers unlock once both edits have played to the end once. Watch them several times if you need to.
  • Please use a laptop or desktop screen. There are 25 comparisons, about 20 minutes. Your progress is saved in this browser.
